Negosyo Naka-Auto

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Negosyo Naka-Auto is a personal automation project operated by Benjie Lipalam. This policy explains the limited information processed when someone uses this portfolio, its contact features, and its TikTok draft-upload integration.

Information processed

The portfolio may process the information you choose to provide through a contact form or chat, such as your name, email address, and message. The portfolio chat also creates a technical session identifier so that a conversation can be kept together.

How information is used

Information is used only to respond to enquiries, operate and improve the portfolio assistant, and maintain the associated automation workflows. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Service providers

The project may use Firebase to store chat events, n8n and Google Gemini to process portfolio-assistant requests, and TikTok's Content Posting API when an authorized creator chooses to upload a video draft. TikTok authorization tokens are used only to perform the action the creator authorized.

TikTok draft uploads

When the TikTok integration is used, an authorized account may send a video draft to TikTok. The creator reviews and chooses whether to publish it in TikTok. The integration does not directly post content unless a separate direct-post permission is explicitly enabled.

Retention and security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect automation credentials and stored messages. Information is retained only for as long as needed to operate the relevant conversation or workflow, subject to the retention settings of the connected services.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you supplied by contacting the operator at lipalambenjie@gmail.com.
